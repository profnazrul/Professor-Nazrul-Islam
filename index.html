About

Profile

Nazrul Islam is a Chair Professor of Business at the University of East London, where he directs the school’s PhD and MPhil programmes and co-directs the Centre of FinTech. His research sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence, big-data analytics, digital transformation, and technology management, with a growing focus on how organisations adopt AI in operations, supply chains, financial services, and the workforce. Over 25 years he has taught and built curriculum — in person, fully online, and hybrid — across six UK universities at BSc, MSc, and MBA level, and has supervised doctoral and professional-doctorate researchers to completion across three institutions. His work has been cited in the White House’s 2024 Economic Report of the President, and he writes and comments for the Financial Times, The Economist, ZDNet, and The Conversation.
